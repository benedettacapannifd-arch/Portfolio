About

Moving between consultancy, collaboration and personal exploration.
I believe in open-ended practices guided by curiosity and the desire
to keep learning through making, adapting and workig together.

With a background in denim and heavy fabrics design, I support brands through
garment conception, pattern cutting and small-scale prototyping.

My hands-on approach combines traditional techniques with a more intuitive,
anarchic way of working, shaped by the uniqueness of each project and the
people involved.

Beyond fashion, I collaborate with artists and creatives to shape environments
and explore the visual coherence of their projects.
